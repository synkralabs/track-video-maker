Private by design. Your audio and photos are read and turned into video inside your own browser. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

Browser support. Chrome and Edge on a computer create MP4 files several times faster than real time. Firefox and Safari record in real time instead, which takes as long as the song and may save a WebM file. Most platforms accept WebM, but MP4 is the safest choice.

Saved in this browser only. Your settings, artist name and default photo are stored on this device, in this browser. They are not shared with other devices or other people.
